Authorised administrator identities

Registration is checked against the server-side AURORA_ADMIN_IDENTITIES configuration. Each person has one approved phone number and one or more approved email addresses. Phone verification and email verification are required before admin sign-in. National ID numbers are stored only as protected hashes. Individual permissions, login MFA, and append-only administrator audit history still require backend implementation.
